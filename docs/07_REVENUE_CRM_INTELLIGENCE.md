# Revenue CRM-lite & Intelligence

## Purpose

Connect acquisition activity to commercial outcomes.

ARO is interested in revenue context, not every generic CRM activity.

## Revenue customer profile

A customer view should answer:
- which projects they interacted with;
- first/last source;
- offers viewed;
- checkout attempts;
- orders;
- products owned;
- refunds/chargebacks;
- total gross/net revenue;
- current entitlements.

## Funnel model

Recommended stages:
- LEAD_CAPTURED
- SALES_PAGE_VIEWED
- OFFER_SELECTED
- CHECKOUT_STARTED
- PAYMENT_CREATED
- PAYMENT_APPROVED
- ENTITLEMENT_GRANTED
- UPSELL_OFFERED
- UPSELL_APPROVED
- REFUND

## Attribution

V1:
- utm_source
- utm_medium
- utm_campaign
- utm_content
- project
- offer
- landing route

Never store private workbook/journal/reflection answers for targeting.

## Core metrics

- leads
- checkout starts
- paid conversion
- checkout abandonment
- gross revenue
- net revenue
- AOV
- bump conversion
- upsell conversion
- refund rate
- provider approval rate
- provider latency
- provider fee rate
- revenue per campaign
- customer LTV

## Agent analysis

Hermes can continuously identify:
- anomalous conversion drops;
- provider degradation;
- fee increases;
- campaign/product revenue shifts;
- refund anomalies;
- country/currency opportunities.

Recommendations must cite the underlying operational metrics and time window.
